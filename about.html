Meet the People Who Make This Happen

Ashley Olsen — Founder & President

Hello everyone! My name is Ashley Olsen, Founder and President of Every Body Wellness, and this photo here is the reason why! Helping people gain access to real guidance and community has been at the heart of everything I do; as a personal trainer, nutrition coach, and community health advocate. My work focuses on serving those often left out of traditional health spaces. Fitness alone isn’t enough. True health means supporting every part of life, from nutrition and sleep to emotional well-being and daily habits. Our mission is to remove barriers, provide support, and empower people with holistic, realistic plans. We don’t just teach workouts; we help build fulfilling, healthy lives—one step at a time. Since December, I’ve led weekly group fitness classes for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities. It’s been deeply rewarding to see confidence grow, community strengthen, and overall well-being improve. The skills and movements practiced in class have helped increase mobility, strength, and quality of life. Too often, underserved members of our community from diverse backgrounds, including those with disabilities are excluded from fitness spaces due to assumptions about what they can or should do. Movement is accessible and essential for everyone, regardless of ability, background, or financial situation. In April, I founded Every Body Wellness to expand this mission and bring inclusive wellness programs to more people. I believe together we can build a stronger, healthier community and create lasting change.
